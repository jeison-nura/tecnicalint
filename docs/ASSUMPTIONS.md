# Assumptions

> The scenario is intentionally incomplete. Record every assumption you needed to make, and the part it affects.

| # | Assumption | Why I needed it | Part(s) affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The durable intake store and its transaction commit are the acceptance boundary: return the provider-required success response only after a verified, normalized notification and its publication intent are durably committed. | The requirements define acceptance by the 2xx response and prohibit losing accepted notifications; the providers have short response deadlines. | A, B, C |
| 2 | Use Azure Service Bus for durable asynchronous delivery and Azure SQL for notification/payment state plus a transactional outbox. The exact service tier and regional redundancy remain subject to cost and IaC validation. | The design needs durable buffering, DLQ, searchable processing state, uniqueness constraints, and safe event publication. | A, C |
| 3 | The canonical business payment identity is country + provider + provider transaction/payment ID; provider event/notification IDs identify delivery/state-change records, not the payment itself. | Andina creates a new notification ID on retries, while other providers may emit multiple events for one payment. | A, B |
| 4 | For equal provider state-change timestamps with conflicting states, no reliable provider sequence is available in the supplied specifications; quarantine for investigation rather than infer an order. | The specifications provide timestamps but no monotonic sequence/version for every provider. | A, B, D |
| 5 | The stated 99.5% within-one-hour freshness objective cannot be guaranteed during a Salesforce outage longer than one hour; Salesforce outages may last up to 90 minutes. Preserve and replay accepted work, report the affected SLO breach, and avoid claiming an impossible guarantee. | Scenario NFR2 and the Salesforce constraints conflict for outages exceeding one hour. | A, D |
| 6 | Store only normalized payment data, provider identifiers, timestamps, processing state and non-sensitive diagnostic metadata; do not persist webhook raw bodies, payer details, or payment tokens after signature verification. | Raw payloads may contain personal data or payment tokens, and the design must minimize sensitive data exposure. | A, B, C, D |
| 7 | Operational notification metadata is retained online for an initial 90 days, subject to confirmation by Legal/Compliance; payment state is retained according to reconciliation and regulatory requirements. | The scenario does not specify retention periods or country-specific retention rules. | A, C, D |
